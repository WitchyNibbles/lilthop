# Code of conduct

Lilthop is made for trans women and transfeminine people, and for anyone who works on it with them.

This is a place to be kind.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here are learning, and some are nervous about their voice.
- Use the name and pronouns people give you.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Say it plainly when you don't know something.

## What we don't allow

- Transphobia, including doubting that someone is who they say they are, and "just asking questions" about it.
- Harassment, insults, threats, or following someone around the project.
- Sharing someone's personal details, recordings or voice without their say.
- Pushing medical advice on people, or telling someone their voice is wrong.

## What happens

The maintainer can remove comments, issues, pull requests and anything else that breaks this, and can ban people from the project.

For serious things, no warning is needed.
